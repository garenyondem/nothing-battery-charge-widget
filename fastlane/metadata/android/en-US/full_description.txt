Show the battery level on the Glyph lights while a Nothing phone charges.

Glyph Charge sends the charge level to the Glyph Interface as a progress
ring while the charger is connected. By default the Glyph starts only when
the phone lies face down and flat, so the lights stay quiet while you use
the phone.

The app also posts a Live Update card in the notification shade. Nothing OS
turns it into Glyph progress while charging.

Features

• Battery level as a Glyph progress ring while charging
• Face-down gate: the Glyph starts only when the phone lies flat and face down
• On-screen preview: a twelve-dot ring with the level, charge state and face state
• Day and night theme, following the system theme by default
• No internet permission. No account, no ads, no tracking

How it works

1. Detect the charger. The app combines the charger state, the current and
the charge trend. The meter starts when the signals agree.
2. Confirm the position. The app reads the screen-upward sensor and samples
gravity for two seconds. The phone must lie flat and still.
3. Send the level. The app posts a Live Update with the battery level. The
update stops when charging stops or you pick the phone up.

Requirements

• A Nothing phone with a Glyph Interface, Nothing OS 4.1 or later
• Notification permission
• Glyph Progress enabled in the Nothing Glyph app (on by default)
• Background access set to Unrestricted for reliable restarts (recommended)

Good to know

• The app keeps a small service notification while the meter runs. Android
needs this notification to keep the meter alive. You can swipe it away.
• A force stop cancels the meter until you open the app again.
• On other Android 16 phones the app installs, but only Nothing phones have
a Glyph Interface.
